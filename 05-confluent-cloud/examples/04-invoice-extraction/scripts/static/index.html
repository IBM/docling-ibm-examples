The Flink pipeline

One long-running statement in Confluent Cloud for Apache Flink: it extracts each released invoice — synthetic or real, by its mode — and carries the extraction on to invoices, EUR and the three reports, writing every table at the same commit. Drawn from the SQL in flink/, the same graph as the README. Live: each statement's phase from the Flink SQL API, record counts from the topics, ⚡ where the Real-Time Context Engine serves the table. Lumen Analytics GmbH

A fictional data company in Munich, growing from about 38 to 54 people in the first half of 2026. The company, its suppliers and every invoice are invented; only the FX rates are real (ECB).

  • Jan sales kickoff in Lisbon
  • Feb–Apr hiring wave
  • Mar trade fair in Amsterdam
  • Apr–May financing round
  • Jun summer party

Department and category are chosen by whoever submits the invoice — the extraction reads only the vendor, the amounts and the line items.

Lightning queries

Millisecond reads of a table's current contents, served by Confluent's Real-Time Context Engine — the same endpoint an AI agent connects to over MCP. Flink computes; the engine serves. No aggregates, no joins, at most 200 rows: anything heavier belongs in a Flink statement.
